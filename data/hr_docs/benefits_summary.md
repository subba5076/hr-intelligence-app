# Employee Benefits Summary

## Health Insurance
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date.
- The company offers three medical plan tiers: Basic PPO, Standard PPO, and High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA.
- Employee-only premiums are fully covered by the company for the Basic PPO plan. Dependent coverage is subsidized at 70%.
- Open enrollment occurs annually in November for coverage effective the following January 1st.

## Retirement
- 401(k) plan is available starting day one, with automatic enrollment at a 4% contribution rate (you may opt out or change this).
- The company matches 100% of the first 4% of contributions and 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum match of 5%.
- Employer match vests immediately (no vesting schedule).

## Paid Time Off
- Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year in years 1-3, increasing to 20 days in years 4+.
- 10 paid company holidays per year, published annually on the HR portal calendar.
- PTO is accrued monthly and can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year.

## Parental Leave
- 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver, 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
- Leave can be taken within 12 months of birth, adoption, or foster placement.

## Wellness and Other Perks
- $1,000 annual wellness stipend (gym memberships, mental health apps, fitness equipment).
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offering free confidential counseling sessions (up to 6 per year).
- $2,000 per year tuition reimbursement for job-related coursework or certifications.
- Life insurance equal to 1x annual salary provided at no cost; supplemental coverage available for purchase.

## How to Enroll
Log in to the Benefits Portal within 30 days of your start date. If you do not make an election, you will default to the Basic PPO plan with employee-only coverage. Questions: benefits@company.example
