# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Welcome
Welcome to the company! This guide covers everything you need during your first 90 days.

## Before Your First Day
- IT will email you a laptop pickup/shipping confirmation 5 business days before your start date.
- Complete your I-9 and W-4 (or local equivalent tax forms) in the Workday portal before day one.
- Your manager will send a calendar invite for a 30-minute welcome call on your first morning.

## Day One
- Arrive (or log in, for remote employees) at 9:00 AM local time.
- HR orientation runs from 9:30 AM to 12:00 PM and covers benefits enrollment, company policies, and workplace tools.
- You will be issued a company badge, laptop, and accounts for email, Slack, and the HR portal.
- IT security training must be completed within the first 3 business days.

## First Week
- Meet with your manager to review your 30/60/90 day plan.
- Complete mandatory compliance training: Code of Conduct, Anti-Harassment, and Data Privacy (deadline: end of week 1).
- Set up direct deposit and review your benefits elections (deadline: 30 days from start date).
- Join your team's recurring meetings and shadow at least one project.

## First 30 Days
- Complete the "New Hire Checklist" in the HR portal.
- Schedule 1:1s with key cross-functional partners identified by your manager.
- Enroll in health, dental, and vision benefits if you have not already (see Benefits Summary document).

## First 90 Days
- Your manager will conduct a 90-day check-in covering performance expectations and career development.
- Probationary period (where applicable by role/region) ends at 90 days.

## Who to Contact
- IT Help Desk: it-support@company.example (equipment, accounts, access issues)
- HR Business Partner: hr-onboarding@company.example (paperwork, benefits, policy questions)
- Payroll: payroll@company.example (pay, direct deposit, tax forms)
