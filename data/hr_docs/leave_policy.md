# Leave of Absence Policy

## Types of Leave
1. **Sick Leave** - Paid, accrued at 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours per year, for personal or family illness.
2. **Bereavement Leave** - Up to 5 paid days for immediate family, 2 paid days for extended family.
3. **Jury Duty** - Full pay continuation for up to 10 business days; documentation from the court required.
4. **Personal Leave of Absence** - Unpaid, up to 30 days, subject to manager and HR approval, for circumstances not covered by other leave types.
5. **Medical Leave (FMLA or local equivalent)** - Up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave for a serious health condition. Requires medical certification submitted to HR within 15 days of the leave request.
6. **Military Leave** - Job-protected leave in accordance with applicable law for employees called to active duty or training.

## Requesting Leave
- Submit a leave request through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leave (no advance notice required for emergencies).
- Your manager and HR Business Partner will both need to approve leave requests exceeding 5 consecutive days.
- Intermittent leave (e.g., recurring medical appointments) must be pre-approved and logged in the HR portal.

## Pay and Benefits During Leave
- Paid leave types (sick, bereavement, jury duty) continue at full pay and do not affect benefits.
- Unpaid leave exceeding 30 days may affect benefits continuation; contact benefits@company.example before your leave begins.
- PTO continues to accrue during paid leave but not during unpaid leave.

## Returning from Leave
- Notify your manager and HR at least 5 business days before your planned return date.
- Employees returning from medical leave longer than 4 weeks may be asked to provide a fitness-for-duty certification.
