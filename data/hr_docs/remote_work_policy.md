# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
- Roles are classified as Remote, Hybrid, or On-site by the hiring manager and noted in the job description.
- Hybrid employees are expected in the office a minimum of 2 days per week, coordinated with their team.

## Equipment and Expenses
- The company provides a laptop and a one-time $500 home office stipend for remote and hybrid employees.
- Internet and phone reimbursement of up to $50/month is available for fully remote employees; submit receipts through the Expense Portal.

## Work Hours and Availability
- Remote employees must be available during their team's core collaboration hours (typically 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time), even if working flexible hours otherwise.
- Update your calendar and status in Slack to reflect availability, including time zone if working across regions.

## Security Requirements
- Remote work must be performed on company-issued devices with VPN and endpoint protection enabled.
- Do not work from public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks without VPN active.
- Home offices should have a private, secure space for handling confidential information and video calls.

## Requesting a Change
Employees who want to change their work arrangement (e.g., relocate, switch from hybrid to remote) must submit a request through the HR portal for manager and HR approval at least 30 days in advance.
