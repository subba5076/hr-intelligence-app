# Code of Conduct

## Our Values
Integrity, respect, accountability, and inclusion guide every decision we make as a company.

## Workplace Conduct
- Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with professionalism and respect.
- Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- Conflicts of interest (financial, familial, or otherwise) must be disclosed to your manager and HR before they affect a business decision.

## Confidentiality and Data
- Confidential company information, including source code, customer data, and financial records, must not be shared outside the company without authorization.
- Employee and customer personal data must be handled in accordance with the Data Privacy Policy and applicable regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc. as relevant).
- Report any suspected data breach to security@company.example immediately.

## Use of Company Resources
- Company equipment, accounts, and systems are provided for business use; incidental personal use is permitted within reason.
- Do not install unauthorized software or connect unapproved devices to company systems.

## Reporting Concerns
- Employees may report concerns to their manager, HR, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline (ethics-hotline@company.example).
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is a violation of this policy and will be addressed immediately.

## Enforcement
Violations of this Code of Conduct are investigated by HR and may result in coaching, formal warnings, suspension, or termination depending on severity.
